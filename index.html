06 / THE MORNING AFTER

The funny stories count.
So do the difficult ones.

Memory gets kinder. Consequences don't always. The archive leaves room for embarrassment, regret, damaged relationships, loneliness, ageing and occasions when drinking stopped being funny.

Not as a sermon. As part of the record. The point is to remember the whole night—not merely the bit worth repeating in the pub.
